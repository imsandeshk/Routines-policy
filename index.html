Privacy Policy

Daily Routines • Your data stays yours

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Daily Routines (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Routines collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

Daily Routines stores routine data created by you, including:

  • Routine names
  • Checklist items
  • Notes and custom blocks
  • Routine schedules
  • Progress and streak history

Device Storage

All routine data is stored locally on your device unless cloud sync is explicitly enabled in a future update.

Notifications

Daily Routines may use notification permissions to send routine reminders scheduled by you.

Analytics

Daily Routines does not collect personal information, sell user data, or share your routine data with third parties.

Third-Party Services

This app currently does not use third-party advertising services.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information stored within the app.

Children's Privacy

Daily Routines is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: support@dailyroutines.app
